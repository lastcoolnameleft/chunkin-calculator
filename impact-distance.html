Impact Distance Tracker

Triangulate trebuchet landing distance from two observation stations — spotters read one angle each, no math required on their end.

1. Setup (once, before the event)

No baseline saved yet.

Stations A and B need a clear view of the entire target area. One pair covers every trebuchet. At each station, zero an angle-sighting device (large protractor on a stake, or a phone sighting app) toward the other station — that's 0°. Compass bearings work too but are less reliable near metal trebuchets.

2. Trebuchets (one-time survey, do for each of the four)

For each trebuchet, measure its distance from Station A and the angle from the A→B baseline to the trebuchet (sighted from A; positive angles sweep into the target area, negative angles down to -180° sweep behind the baseline).
